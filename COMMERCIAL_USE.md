# Commercial Use of SXS

SXS is source-available software owned by Rasya Andrean and Science Experimental Technologies. It is not distributed under an open-source license.

The controlling terms are in [LICENSE](LICENSE). This page is a practical summary and does not replace them.

## When the royalty applies

Commercial use generally includes a paid product, hosted service, consulting engagement, analysis deliverable, internal revenue-generating workflow, or other commercial project in which SXS or an SXS-generated output is a material component.

The standard royalty is **10% of Covered Revenue**, as defined in the license. Commercial users must register the use before it begins, report quarterly, and pay within 30 days after each calendar quarter.

Non-commercial research, education, personal evaluation, and community contribution do not incur the royalty when they remain non-commercial and comply with the attribution and redistribution terms.

## Required attribution

Public projects and outputs must display:

> Built with SXS — SCIX Exoplanet Search, created by Rasya Andrean under Science Experimental Technologies.

Where links are supported, link the project name to:

`https://github.com/Science-Experimental-Technologies/Exoplanet-Search`

Academic publications must also cite the software using [CITATION.cff](CITATION.cff). Award and competition submissions materially enabled by SXS must acknowledge SXS, Rasya Andrean, and Science Experimental Technologies where the rules permit.

## Registration and alternative agreements

Before commercial deployment, email `scix.official@gmail.com` with:

- the legal name and contact details of the user or organization;
- a short description of the product, service, or project;
- the planned start date and revenue model; and
- the billing and royalty-reporting contact.

A separately signed commercial agreement may define alternative reporting, payment, support, branding, or enterprise terms.

## Important legal note

The repository changed from MIT licensing after public release `v1.0.0`. Copies already obtained under that earlier release retain the rights granted with those copies. The current license governs only revisions that carry it. Obtain qualified legal advice before relying on these terms for material commercial activity.
